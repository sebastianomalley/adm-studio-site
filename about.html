Where it started

Real people, real stories

My background is documentary film, and that's still how I come at every project. Whether it's a headshot or a fundraising piece, I'm looking for the true version of the person in front of the lens. I've also worked as an actor, so I know what it feels like on the other side of the camera.

New York

Years behind the scenes

More than a decade of high-profile events in New York, including helping launch Soho House Los Angeles, and filming new exhibits for the American Museum of Natural History. Events taught me to stay calm when the timeline slips.

Teaching

Helping people do their best work

I taught in public schools in North Carolina, Boston, and New York City, including an after-school film and theater club. I'd still rather explain why something works than just hand over a file.

Now

The camera and the code

After a full-stack bootcamp and coursework at Harvard, I started building websites and apps alongside the film work. Outside client work I'm Creative Director for the Mary McGarry Children's Foundation, and I've produced fundraising work for CURE Epilepsy.
