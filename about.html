Where it started

Real people, real stories

My background is in documentary film, and that's still how I come at every project. Sugar Rat Productions, the studio's film division, is where that work lives. Whether it's a headshot, a music video, or a fundraising piece, I'm looking for the true version of the person or the business in front of the lens.

I've also spent time as an actor, so I know what it feels like on the other side of the camera. That shapes how I run a shoot: relaxed, unhurried, and in natural light whenever I can get it.

New York

Years behind the scenes

I spent more than a decade in New York working high-profile events and hospitality. At Soho House I worked in food and beverage and in the cinema department, and I was one of five people from the New York house sent to help launch Soho House Los Angeles. Later I worked on high-end events and runway shows around the city.

Along the way I filmed new exhibits for the American Museum of Natural History, footage that was shared with partner museums around the world, and photographed for Soho House. Events taught me to stay calm when the timeline slips and to notice the details nobody else is watching.

Teaching

Helping people do their best work

Before New York, I worked in public schools in North Carolina, Boston, and New York City, including running an after-school film and theater club. Teaching is still part of how I work with clients. I'd rather explain why something works than just hand over a file.

Now

The camera and the code

After a full-stack development bootcamp and coursework at Harvard, I started building websites and apps alongside the film work. I'm now about halfway through a master's in Digital Media Design at Harvard Extension School, and I hold Scrum Master and Product Owner certifications, so projects come with a real plan and a clear timeline.

Outside of client work, I'm Creative Director and a board member for the Mary McGarry Children's Foundation, which supports kids in Central Uganda, and I've produced fundraising work for CURE Epilepsy.
