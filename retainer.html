Is this the right fit?

Retainer vs. one-off

Not every project needs an ongoing partner. Here's the honest breakdown.

Choose retainer if
You need content and site updates regularly, not just once, and want one person who already knows your brand handling it.
Choose one-off if
You need a single shoot, a one-time site build, or a project with a clear start and end. Photography sessions are priced on my rates page. Everything else gets a quote based on scope.
Not sure?
Start with a one-off project. If it's a good fit on both sides, moving to a retainer later is easy.
