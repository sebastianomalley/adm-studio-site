Monthly Retainer Agreement

This agreement is between A Digital Media Studio, LLC, represented by Jason O'Malley (the "Studio"), and the client named in the signature section (the "Client"). The plan and price the Client selects above form Schedule A.

1. Services and term

1.1 Services. Each month the Studio provides the services in the selected plan (the "Monthly Plan"). The mix may shift month to month, as long as the total work stays within the plan.

1.2 Planning. Each month starts with a planning session to set priorities. Work not agreed in planning is scheduled for a later month or billed as out-of-scope work.

1.3 Term. The agreement starts on the start date and runs for an initial term of three (3) months, then continues month to month.

1.4 Ending the agreement. After the initial term, either party may end the agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice; email is fine. The Client pays for the notice period, and the Studio delivers the work scheduled for it.

1.5 Ending early. If the Client ends the agreement during the initial term, the remaining months of that term are due. Either party may end it immediately if the other seriously breaches it and does not fix the breach within ten (10) days of written notice.

1.6 Pause. After the initial term, the Client may pause once per year, for up to one (1) month, with thirty (30) days' written notice. No fee is due for the paused month.

2. Fees and payment

2.1 Monthly fee. The Client pays the monthly fee for the selected plan. The first month is due before work begins. Registered nonprofits receive 10% off when noted.

2.2 Invoices. The Studio invoices on the 1st of each month. Invoices are due within seven (7) days by card, ACH or check.

2.3 Late payment. A balance unpaid fifteen (15) days after its due date incurs a late fee of 1.5% per month, or the highest rate the law allows if lower. The Studio may pause work until the account is current.

2.4 Out-of-scope work. Work beyond the Monthly Plan is billed at the add-on rates shown above. The Studio quotes it and gets the Client's approval, by email or otherwise in writing, before starting.

2.5 Unused hours. Unused hours do not roll over and are not refunded.

2.6 Expenses. Travel more than 30 miles from Washington, DC, location or permit fees, props, and paid stock or music are billed at cost, with the Client's approval first.

2.7 Rescheduling. The Client may reschedule a shoot at no charge with at least 48 hours' notice. A shoot cancelled with less notice counts as delivered for that month, unless it can be rebooked the same month. The Studio reschedules at no cost if it must cancel, including for illness or unsafe weather.

2.8 Price changes. The Studio may change the monthly fee with sixty (60) days' written notice, and not during the initial term.

3. Deliverables and revisions

3.1 Turnaround. Edited photos within five (5) business days of a shoot, videos within seven to ten (7–10) business days, and website changes within three (3) business days of a complete request. Rush delivery is available at 25% extra.

3.2 Delivery. Photos and videos are delivered by online gallery or download link in web- and social-ready formats. Raw, unedited files are not included unless agreed in writing.

3.3 Revisions. Each photo set and video includes one (1) round of revisions, requested in one list within seven (7) days of delivery. Further rounds are out-of-scope work. A new concept, reshoot or major change of direction is new work.

3.4 Client responsibilities. The Client gives timely feedback and access to people, locations and content; provides logins needed for website work; has the right to use any logos, music, text or images it supplies; and arranges permission to shoot on its premises or at its events. Delays caused by missing feedback or materials extend delivery dates by the same amount.

4. Ownership and use

4.1 License. Once paid for, the Client receives a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use the delivered photos and videos for its own marketing: website, social media, email, print, presentations and organic promotion.

4.2 Separate licenses. Paid advertising campaigns, broadcast, billboards, resale or licensing to third parties are not included and are quoted separately.

4.3 Copyright. The Studio keeps the copyright in all photos, video and footage, including unused material. The Client may not sell, sublicense or transfer the work, or alter it beyond cropping and resizing in a way that misrepresents it.

4.4 Websites. Once paid for, the Client owns the website content and pages built for it. The Studio keeps its reusable code, templates and tools, and grants the Client a perpetual license to use them as part of its site.

4.5 Accounts. The Client owns its domain, hosting and social accounts. The Studio uses access only for this agreement and returns or deletes credentials when it ends.

4.6 Portfolio. The Studio may show the work and the Client's name and logo in its portfolio and on social media, unless the Client opts out above.

4.7 Releases. The Client is responsible for getting consent from its staff and guests who appear in the work, for the uses it intends. The Studio provides a model release form on request.

4.8 Storage. The Studio keeps delivered files for at least twelve (12) months after delivery.

5. Legal terms

5.1 Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's non-public business information confidential and uses it only for this agreement.

5.2 Independent contractor. The Studio is an independent contractor. It sets its own methods and hours, provides its own equipment, and handles its own taxes and insurance.

5.3 Limit of liability. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, such as lost profits. The Studio's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the three (3) months before a claim. If files are lost or damaged through the Studio's fault, its responsibility is limited to reshooting at no charge or refunding the fee for the affected work.

5.4 Indemnity. The Client is responsible for claims arising from materials it supplies or from use of the work outside this agreement. The Studio is responsible for claims that its original work infringes someone else's rights.

5.5 Events outside our control. Neither party is responsible for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as illness, severe weather, outages or government action. Affected work is rescheduled.

5.6 Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the District of Columbia. The parties will first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion for thirty (30) days.

5.7 Entire agreement. This agreement and the selected plan are the full agreement. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both parties; email counts. If any part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.

5.8 Electronic signature. Both parties agree that typed names submitted through this page are valid signatures. The Studio countersigns by emailing a confirmation of the start date.
