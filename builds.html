From archive to adventure.

Every day NASA publishes a stunning space photo, explained by an astronomer. But exploring them meant clicking through the archive one link at a time. So I turned it into a smooth, scrollable gallery, then turned that gallery into the controls for a game. Pick a photo, learn what you’re looking at, and fly through it. Something useful became something people want to play with, and they learn along the way.

Step 01

Choose your backgrounds

Scroll through NASA’s Astronomy Picture of the Day and pick a favorite for each of the game’s three levels. Close the gallery when you’re done.

Step 02

Play Defender

Your picks appear behind each level. Arrow keys to fly, spacebar to fire, and a boss waiting at the end.

Why it’s here

What’s the “NASA archive” in your business?

Most businesses have something that works but isn’t much fun to use: a resource, a process, a way clients interact with you. I look for ways to turn those into experiences people actually enjoy, whether that’s a tool for your clients, a fresh way to show off your work, or something that makes learning feel like play. If you have an idea that needs a little creative thinking, let’s talk about it.

Defender is built for keyboard play, so open it on a laptop or desktop. A touch version for phones is in development.
